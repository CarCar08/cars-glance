C.A.R.S. GLANCE

Your day at a glance.

A frosted-glass panel in the middle of your Windows desktop: the time and weather, what's playing, your calendar and tasks, and the countdowns you care about. It sits under your windows, never takes focus, and goes still while you game.

Download for Windows Source on GitHub

Now Playing

Cover, title and progress for any player, with previous, play-pause and next.

Agenda

Today's events and your Todoist tasks, in the order that matters.

Next up

Fifteen minutes before an event, the panel counts you in.

Countdowns

Pin the launch, trip or deadline you're waiting for.

Free and open source (MIT). No account, no telemetry. Installs for you only, no admin needed, and updates itself.
Weather data by Open-Meteo.com. Part of the C.A.R.S. family with C.A.R.S. Control.
